## 2. CRITICAL GAPS IDENTIFIED

### 2.1 Missing Critical Features (Must Have)

**1. Credit Terms System** 🔴
- **Market Standard:** Net 30, Net 60, Net 90 payment terms
- **Why Critical:** B2B buyers expect credit, not immediate payment
- **Impact:** Without this, we're not a true B2B marketplace
- **Priority:** 🔴 CRITICAL - Must implement

**2. Approval Workflows** 🔴
- **Market Standard:** Multi-level purchase approvals
- **Why Critical:** B2B companies need approval processes
- **Impact:** Enterprise buyers require this
- **Priority:** 🔴 HIGH - Important for enterprise

**3. RFQ (Request for Quotation)** 🟡
- **Market Standard:** Request quotes from multiple suppliers
- **Why Important:** Custom/bulk orders need quotes
- **Impact:** Attracts large buyers
- **Priority:** 🟡 MEDIUM - Can add later

**4. Variable Weight Products** 🟡
- **F&B Specific:** Meat, cheese priced by weight
- **Why Important:** Essential for F&B products
- **Impact:** Needed for F&B vertical
- **Priority:** 🟡 MEDIUM - F&B requirement

**5. Supplier Ratings** 🟡
- **Market Standard:** Rate suppliers, not just products
- **Why Important:** Builds trust
- **Impact:** Helps buyers choose suppliers
- **Priority:** 🟡 MEDIUM - Trust building

## 8. KEY DECISIONS NEEDED

### 8.1 Critical Decisions

**1. Credit Terms Implementation**
- **Question:** How to handle credit risk?
- **Options:**
  - A) Platform guarantees (we take risk)
  - B) Merchant guarantees (merchant takes risk)
  - C) Third-party credit (credit company)
- **Recommendation:** Start with merchant guarantees, add platform guarantee later

**2. Approval Workflow Complexity**
- **Question:** How complex should approvals be?
- **Options:**
  - A) Simple (single approver)
  - B) Medium (multi-level)
  - C) Complex (conditional, rules-based)
- **Recommendation:** Start with medium, add complexity later

**3. RFQ Priority**
- **Question:** Is RFQ critical for launch?
- **Options:**
  - A) Yes, must have for launch
  - B) No, can add in Phase 2
- **Recommendation:** Can add in Phase 2, not critical for MVP

**4. AI Features Priority**
- **Question:** Should AI be in MVP?
- **Options:**
  - A) Yes, differentiator
  - B) No, add later
- **Recommendation:** Add later, focus on core B2B first
